Privacy Policy — Last One Pays for Telegram

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Last One Pays for Telegram (the Last One Pays bot and its Mini App) is operated by Catalyst Growth SG Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). This policy describes what it collects, why, who can see it, and how long it is kept. It describes the bot as it actually behaves. The iOS app has its own policy.

The short version

  • Your location is collected only when you choose to share your Live Location with the bot, and it is only used for meetups you have joined, from 1 hour before the meet time until 6 hours after it.
  • Only the people in the same meetup can see where you are, and only during that window.
  • We keep your meetup history, including the location fixes you shared, until you delete it. Send /deletemydata to the bot at any time.
  • We never sell your data, show ads, or handle money. Tabs are settled between friends.

We only accept Live Location from your own phone. Static pins, venues and forwarded locations are refused and not stored. If you share a Live Location when you are not in a meetup whose live window is open, the bot does not store it.

Who can see your data

  • People in the same meetup see your name, vehicle, position and ETA while its live map is open, and your arrival time and the result afterwards.
  • A linked group chat sees join, arrival and result messages for that meetup.
  • Anyone with a meetup's invite link sees its title, place, time, host's name, number of people and the stakes, but not who is going or where anyone is.

Location history is the most sensitive thing we hold. It is never shown to anyone after a meetup's live window closes, but it is retained. If that is not acceptable to you, delete your data.

Deleting your data

Send /deletemydata to the bot, then /deletemydata confirm. This immediately deletes your profile, every location fix you shared, your memberships, results and tabs. Meetups you host are deleted for everyone in them. You can also email sharveenkumar@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days.

Safety

ETAs are estimates for planning, never a reason to hurry. Follow traffic rules, and if you are driving, check updates only when stopped or ask a passenger.

Children

Last One Pays is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Email sharveenkumar@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the bot.
